# Collision Battlestar Map Reference Research

Date: 2026-09-29

The rebuild uses representative visual references from current Roblox battleground and wave-survival experiences. The implementation is procedural and does not bundle third-party gameplay assets.

## Visual directions extracted

- Battleground spaces favor a readable combat plane, strong landmarks, short sightline breaks, and controlled verticality.
- Current arena examples use ramps, elevated platforms, industrial framing, and clear boundary structures without making the center unreadable.
- Wave-survival examples emphasize obvious spawn lanes and points, a visible wave state, open kite space, and repeated encounter geometry.
- Dark or sci-fi references use restrained accent lighting rather than filling the whole arena with neon.
- Mobile-first layouts benefit from wide center space and obstacles that do not hide small characters behind dense scenery.

## Resulting map language

Collision Battlestar uses a compact rectangular combat district around a circular Collision Core, four low cover clusters, six enemy gates, four ruined frames, two raised step pockets, a southern upgrade station, and long outer walls. The geometry is intentionally self-authored and procedural so gameplay logic does not depend on Toolbox scripts or asset packages.
