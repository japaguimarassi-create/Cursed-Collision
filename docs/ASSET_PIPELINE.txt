IMPORT SAFETY
-------------
- Third-party models are sanitized by MapAssetLoader before they enter the world.
- Imported model scripts and module scripts are removed.
- Imported BaseParts are anchored and have CanTouch/CanQuery disabled.
- Asset loading runs after the procedural map exists and is best-effort only.
- If external loading fails, the procedural map remains complete.
- Never add an unknown AnimationId or SoundId merely to fill a slot.
