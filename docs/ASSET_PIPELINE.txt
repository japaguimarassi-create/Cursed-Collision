IMPORT SAFETY
-------------
- Third-party models are sanitized by MapAssetLoader before they enter the world.
- Imported model scripts and module scripts are removed.
- Imported BaseParts are anchored and have CanTouch/CanQuery disabled.
- Asset loading runs after the procedural map exists and is best-effort only.
- If external loading fails, the procedural map remains complete.
- Never add an unknown AnimationId or SoundId merely to fill a slot.

ANIMATION
Public/free Creator Store animation packs were verified, including an R6 punch-fighting pack and an R6 punch
animation. Their public pages expose model/pack IDs, not a child Animation asset ID that can be safely copied
into AnimationDefinitions.lua. The active source also does not declare the player's rig type. Therefore all
12 runtime animation IDs remain empty and the existing procedural Motor6D fallback is deliberately retained.
Sources:
https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/75164220659481/Fists-Combat-Animation-Pack-R6-Punch-Fighting
https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/13081191834/R6-Punch-Animation
Official rig guidance:
https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/use-case-tutorials/animation/create-an-animation

PLATFORM RIGHTS
Roblox states that the Creator Store contains free-to-use audio and that third-party asset loading through
AssetService requires the experience setting "Allow Loading Third Party Assets". MapAssetLoader remains best-effort;
the procedural map is created first and does not depend on remote loading.
