Planning conclusions:
- server-side validation is mandatory for state-changing remotes;
- StreamingEnabled is appropriate for a large procedural world, but streamed Workspace instances must be treated as dynamic;
- PathfindingService plus modifiers/links is the planned NPC navigation base;
- collection tags are suitable for scalable entity classification;
- Highlight is suitable for the red elite/tagger marker;
- DataStore UpdateAsync is the planned persistence write primitive;
- Roblox's official NPC Kit is a safer first free-asset source than arbitrary toolbox models;
- current Roblox discovery materials emphasize engagement, retention, social interaction and repeat play, so the later game loop is designed around short readable waves plus persistent progression rather than a single endless round.

Current trend signals observed during the research window included large audiences for roleplay, RPG, shooter and survival experiences, with Jujutsu Shenanigans and The Strongest Battlegrounds visible among major current experiences. These observations are treated as descriptive market context, not as a formula for success.
